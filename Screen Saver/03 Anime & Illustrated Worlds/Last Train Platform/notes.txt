- cursor leaves -> after ~2.6 s the eyes close again

Steam train (added 27 Oct 2026; motion approved as preview v0.3, details as v0.4):
- one 3D 1800s train (4-4-0 style engine, tender, two maroon coaches with lit windows), drawn live in code (train.js)
- rails traced in the painting and fitted to a simple camera (horizon y 1235, eye 1.42 m above the rails,
  focal 3400 px on the 3840x2160 painting) -> a centre-line path in metres; each car rides the path on its two axles
- comes out of the haze far down the line, follows the curve, stops beside the platform (~14 s, steam + smoke),
  then rolls on past the camera until the last coach has gone; next train 2-3.5 min later (first ~30 s after start)
- wheels turn with distance travelled, rods follow the cranks; smoke/steam stay where puffed; headlamp glare,
  beam through the rain, glints on the wet rails, window and lamp reflections on the wet ground
- the cat wakes and watches the train's lamp while it is in
- wallpaper: click the green signal or the tracks = call the train; click the waiting train = whistle and leave
