Lighthouse Cove — Anime & Illustrated Worlds
Idea first proposed 26 Oct 2026 as "Pixel Lighthouse Cove" (Pixel Art & Retro); Hima asked for anime film style, so it moved here.
Approved by Hima: idea + picture 26 Oct 2026; v0.1 preview approved 26 Oct 2026.
Usage terms: Figma AI terms — https://www.figma.com/legal/ai-terms/ (checked 26 Oct 2026).

Motion (code, canvas 2D over the picture):
- beam sweeps round every 16 s, soft flash when it faces the viewer; brushes the water far out
- glints on the moon's path and open sea; slow wash on the shore
- stars twinkle (bright points found in the picture), moon glow breathing
- chimney smoke, warm window glow
Beam is drawn on a quarter-resolution layer and scaled up for soft edges.

Wallpaper interaction (no on-screen instructions):
- mouse moves on the desktop -> the beam slowly turns to the cursor and lights the water there (extra warm glints)
- ~4 s after the mouse stops -> back to sweeping
Screensaver mode: beam only sweeps.

Key positions (source pixels in 3840x2160): lantern 2880,352 · moon 1110,450 · chimney 3472,600 · horizon y 1105 · sea x < 2250/2500/2900 (y <1300/<1500/<1720)
