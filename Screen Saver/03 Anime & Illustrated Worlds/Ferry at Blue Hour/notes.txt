- ferry-at-blue-hour-mask.png (640x360): R water, G wake, B beyond the rail (not the boat), A warm lights
- ferry-at-blue-hour-land.png (960x540): R town, G left headland, B middle hill (the shores that move)

  wake/reflections

Motion (WebGL shader + 2D canvas):
- the deck, rail, bench and lamp roll and heave against a level horizon
- the water and its waves slide away from the stern; live foam wake (painted shape, code foam streaming away)
- one crossing = 8 min: the shores shrink toward their far ends as the ferry sails away (nearer = faster),
  the course wanders so they glide sideways at different speeds; reflections drawn from the shores;
  at the end a sea mist covers the horizon (~30 s), the next crossing starts behind it, and it clears
- lamp flicker + warm light pool; glints; town lights breathe; stars (more at night); 3 gulls in code
- time of day from the PC clock (dusk = the painting)

Wallpaper interaction (no on-screen instructions):
- move across the sky: a gull comes to glide beside the hand; rest still: it circles the hand;
  leave the sky: it drifts back out over the bay; click: it flaps and lifts
Screensaver mode ignores the interaction. Reduced motion: one still frame (60 s into a crossing).
