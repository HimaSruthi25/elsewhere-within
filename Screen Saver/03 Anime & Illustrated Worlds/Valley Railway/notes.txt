Valley Railway - Anime & Illustrated Worlds (library scene: valley-railway)
Simulation idea S3 (5 Nov 2026), approved by Hima 5 Nov (preview v0.1, Artifact 8dJnfAb5aNntFgdqPEmSHk).

What moves
- The painted train was removed (background filled, viaduct deck and rails rebuilt). A train (engine + 3 green/cream coaches, drawn in code as 2.5D boxes with lit windows) runs one loop traced on the painted rails: out of the tunnel, across the viaduct, round the left station (stops ~12 s), through the village, over the level crossing and the bridge, up the right curve and back into the tunnel (waits 6-22 s inside). Speeds up and brakes smoothly; smoke from the engine.
- A red two-car shuttle runs back and forth on the right-hand station track and stops at the platform.
- Level crossing: the barrier arm lowers and the red lamps blink while the train passes; cars (cut from the painting, some recoloured) queue at the stop line, keep their distance and drive on.
- The ferry (cut from the painting) crosses the river and waits at each end; wake and funnel smoke.
- Waterfalls and rapids shimmer; town and window lights twinkle.
- Time of day (PC clock): the picture is dusk; dawn tint, day a little warmer, night deep blue with the painted lights restored and train/car lights glowing.

Live wallpaper (hover only)
- Hover a station: its next train waits longer there and the platform lamps and carriage windows glow brighter.
