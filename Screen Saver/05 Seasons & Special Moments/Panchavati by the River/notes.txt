Panchavati by the River — Seasons & Special Moments
Hima's own idea, 13 Nov 2026: she sent a reference picture of Rama, Sita and Lakshmana in the forest and asked
for a living-world wallpaper, "a bit more aesthetic". She chose a painted picture with life on top and the moment
"Panchavati hut by the river". v0.1 (first picture): "ram's face is distorted". v0.2 (new picture, figures bent
in code): "it doesn't look good...can we make it into video". v0.3 (her Gemini video as the base): she asked to
make the wallpaper with that first video.

Picture: Figma AI generate_image (gpt-image-2.5-sunburst), 13 Nov 2026, file se4kqzsYVbGfU5oAcqbQCV node 44:2.
Native 1536x864 (panchavati-by-the-river-source.jpg in this folder). A new composition, not a copy of the
reference picture. Scene picture: resized to 1920x1080 (Lanczos + light unsharp; no AI upscaler).
Usage terms: Figma AI terms (https://www.figma.com/legal/ai-terms/) — check before a public release.

Video: made by Hima in Google Gemini from the 1920x1080 picture with the prompt in prompt.txt
("panch_vatika.mp4": 1280x720, 24 fps, 10 s, with sound). The Gemini sparkle mark at the bottom right is in
the video and was left in (rule: never remove watermarks). Usage terms: Google Gemini / Veo terms — check
before a public release.
Loop made in Python (OpenCV) + ffmpeg: the clip's end does not match its start, so the figures' side (right of
the banyan trunk, soft mask) plays forward then backward with eased turnarounds, and the left side (sky, river,
deer, lotus, falling leaves) plays forward only with a 1 s dissolve at its loop point. 432 frames = 18 s,
enlarged to 1920x1080 (Lanczos + light unsharp), silent, H.264 CRF 24, 8.9 MB
(library/scenes/panchavati-by-the-river/panchavati-by-the-river.mp4).

Masks made in Python from the picture (960x540, alpha only): -water.png (river), -sky.png (open sky).

Motion: the video (figures breathe, blink, turn their heads; Sita strings the garland; river, deer, leaves, lamp).
In code on a 2D canvas over it: light by the PC clock (dawn mist, day as filmed, deeper dusk, blue night with
stars, a moon where the sun was, fireflies, lamp and hut glow), sun rays, bird flocks, an egret, butterflies,
a fish rising now and then.
Live wallpaper (hover only): move over the river -> ripples; rest on the water -> a wider ring; rest on the
bank -> flower petals drift down.
Reduced motion: the still picture, no video. While the host freezes the frame loop, the video is paused.

Checked in the cloud only (13 Nov 2026): eslint, npm test, test:library, build:library, built site headless in
screensaver and wallpaper mode (host pointer move, freeze/unfreeze) with no errors. Not seen on Windows;
speed and video playback in the WebView2 host unchecked. First scene with a video file: adds about 9 MB to the
installer.
